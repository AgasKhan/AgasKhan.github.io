## The game

A 2D action-platformer (run-and-gun) set in a mossy swamp. The demo ships with its own menu (play, donations, credits, quit) and a level flow with fade transitions — a scoped demo of a larger game.

## Mechanics

I implemented the core mechanics in Construct 2:

- **Animated character states**: idle, run, jump, fall and attack.
- **Ranged combat**: shooting with ammo management.
- **Line-of-sight enemy AI** (spiders included), a health system and a HUD.

## Atmosphere and presentation

- **Dynamic lighting** for the dark swamp: lights and shadows via ShadowCaster and penumbra.
- **Physics, scrolling camera** and sound design (footsteps, gunfire, background loop and effects).

## Packaging

I exported the project as a **self-contained HTML5/JavaScript package**, running on the Construct 2 runtime:

- **Mobile-ready** in landscape orientation.
- **Offline support** via Web App Manifest + service worker (PWA).

## What it shows

- **Self-authored gamedev**, from concept to finished demo.
- **Real commercial validation**: the demo was sold.
- Command of the **web game pipeline**: HTML5 export, mobile packaging and offline distribution.
